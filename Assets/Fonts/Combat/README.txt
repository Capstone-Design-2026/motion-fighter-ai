Battle announcement font: Trade Winds by Sideshow (Font Diner, Inc.).
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tradewinds
The unmodified TradeWinds-Regular.ttf is bundled at Assets/Resources/UI/Fonts.
The accompanying TradeWinds-OFL.txt contains its copyright and SIL Open Font License 1.1.

CombatAnnouncementStyle applies the screen-space lean, gradient, outline and shadow.
Only READY, FIGHT and the main battle result use this font. Korean prompts use the regular HUD font.
